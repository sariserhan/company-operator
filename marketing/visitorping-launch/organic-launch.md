## Reddit — r/SideProject / reddit_sideproject

VisitorPing - Live website visitors and iPhone notifications

I built VisitorPing for website owners who want to notice visits as they happen.

It shows visitor activity in a live web dashboard and sends notifications to your iPhone. It works with SaaS websites, WordPress pages, Shopify stores and other supported sites.

The demo uses example data. Visitors are anonymous by default; it doesn’t reveal someone’s name just because they visited.

The product is working, but reaching the people who want it is the part I’m figuring out. If you run a website, would you use the live dashboard, the phone notifications, or both?
