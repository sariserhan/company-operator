# One page. Your audience’s sentence.

A five-minute preview segment for a creator’s stream, short video or social post.

**The prompt:** “What would you put on a web page that somebody else can take over?”

Viewers can suggest a sentence in your comments and explore the preview without buying anything. This is an invitation for an unpaid collaboration. There is no confirmed host or event date yet.

## Five-minute outline

| Time | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:30 | Explain the idea: Take The Wall is one public page with one featured owner. A new paid takeover replaces the previous content. |
| 0:30–1:00 | Show the supplied 15-second actual-app preview, or open the preview yourself. The supplied clip uses example content and shows no purchase. |
| 1:00–3:30 | Ask for sentences viewers are happy to have shown. Preview up to three suggestions, crediting authors only with their agreement. For a recorded short, invite replies afterward instead. |
| 3:30–4:30 | Switch between the desktop and mobile previews. Ask which suggestion people would choose. Replies express preferences; they do not allocate the wall or enter a contest. |
| 4:30–5:00 | Share the public preview link. Explain that publishing is optional and paid. Close with your own opinion of the experiment. |

## What the collaboration includes

Take The Wall supplies the clip, image, outline, copy and a creator-specific link. A creator can run the segment independently; no separate event host, app access or purchase is needed for the preview. With permission, Take The Wall can share the resulting public post from its own account. No audience size or traffic result is promised.

The live wall, checkout, admin tools and buyer inbox are outside this preview segment. Suggestions are not published automatically. No free takeover, reserved slot, prize or payment is offered. A live purchase event would need separate coordination and verified production activation.
