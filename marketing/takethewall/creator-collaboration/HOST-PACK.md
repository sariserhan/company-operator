# One page. Your audience’s sentence.

A five-minute preview segment for a creator’s stream, short video or social post.

**The prompt:** “What would you put on a web page that somebody else can take over?”

Viewers can suggest a sentence in your comments and explore the preview without buying anything. This is an invitation for an unpaid collaboration. There is no confirmed host or event date yet.

| Time | What to do |

## What the collaboration includes

Take The Wall supplies the image, outline, copy and a creator-specific link. A creator can run the segment independently; no separate event host, app access or purchase is needed for the preview. With permission, Take The Wall can share the resulting public post from its own account. No audience size or traffic result is promised.

The live wall, checkout, admin tools and buyer inbox are outside this preview segment. Suggestions are not published automatically. The segment itself does not allocate a takeover, reserve a slot or award a prize. The separate public free-entry route and reward program follow the current Reward Rules. A live purchase event would need separate coordination and verified production activation.
