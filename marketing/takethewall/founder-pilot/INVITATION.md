# Three founders. One shared billboard.

I’m looking for three founders to try a small Take The Wall experiment: put your project on the wall, share your own referral link, and see whether the idea gives people a reason to visit.

This is an early experiment, with no promised audience or sales. There is one live wall; every new activated takeover replaces the previous one.

**No purchase is required to participate.** You can use the official free email-entry route, subject to review. Paid checkout is optional at $4.99 USD plus applicable tax. Neither route reserves a number or guarantees time on the wall.

## What participating involves

1. [Try your project in the free preview](https://www.takethewall.com/?take=1&utm_source=github&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=three_founders&utm_content=invitation).
2. Email **contact@takethewall.com**, subject **Founder pilot**, with your project URL and a sentence about who it is for. This expresses interest; it is not a processed free entry or a confirmed slot.
3. Once we agree on participation, use the official entry process. For free entry: email **contact@takethewall.com**, subject **Free wall entry**, with your name, email, public display name, and desired ad text (up to 120 characters) or URL. Do not send identity documents. Entries are reviewed in received order; paid activations can occur while review is pending.
4. After activation, share the referral link from your owner dashboard wherever your community welcomes it. Sharing is voluntary, does not require a positive review, and does not change entry eligibility.

We’ll agree on a 48-hour sharing window after three participants are confirmed. This is a campaign window, not 48 hours of ownership, a reserved activation schedule, or a reward deadline. The global wall remains open to everyone.

## The existing rewards are separate

At public milestone #100, Reward A is $100 for the qualifying milestone placement and Reward B is a separate $100 for the preceding cohort’s verified referral leader (#1–99). At least one verified referral is needed for Reward B. The cutoff is milestone activation, not the end of this pilot. Claims, eligibility and verification apply; neither reward is guaranteed. This pilot has no additional prize.

[Read the current Reward Rules and free-entry instructions](https://www.takethewall.com/?info=rewards).

No participant names, testimonials or results will be published without permission. Three pilot spaces describe our coordination capacity, not limited availability of the public site or free-entry route.
