## Reddit / SideProject candidate

Title: Would three founders try sharing one internet billboard?

I’m building Take The Wall: one public page where a project or message stays until the next activated takeover replaces it.

I’d like to run a small pilot with three founders. Each would preview their project, activate a placement through the paid or official free-entry route, then optionally share their owner-dashboard referral link. I want to learn whether people share because the wall is interesting, rather than because I keep posting about it.

There’s no promised audience, minimum display time or sales. No purchase is required: valid free entries are reviewed by email. Paid checkout is optional at $4.99 plus applicable tax. The proposed 48-hour sharing window does not reserve the wall or award a separate prize.

Preview and details: https://www.takethewall.com/?take=1&utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=three_founders&utm_content=pilot

Interested founders can reply with their project or email contact@takethewall.com with subject Founder pilot. This is an expression of interest, not automatic entry. Rules and free-entry instructions: https://www.takethewall.com/?info=rewards
