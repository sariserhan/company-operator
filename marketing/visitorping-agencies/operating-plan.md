# VisitorPing acquisition operating plan

Goal: $1,000,000 USD MRR, interpreted literally from “1000K”. Local operator objective updated and read back on September 10, 2026. No deadline or revenue guarantee. Current acquisition advertising/tool budget: $0. Existing affiliate commissions are a cost of referred revenue, not free economics.

## First experiment

Hypothesis: an agency already maintaining client websites can introduce VisitorPing to several relevant owners. These ten organizations are researched candidates, not confirmed buyers or qualified leads. High/medium/low fit is our judgment based on the cited public services, not a measured conversion score.

Initial milestones: three activated agency pilots and one paying account. Do not scale outreach solely because messages were delivered. After ten appropriate delivered inquiries, examine actual replies before another batch. No replies means revisit fit and offer. Trials without activation means investigate that specific experience. Activated trials without payment means interview the users about value and pricing; do not assume checkout is broken.

## Offer

Existing 14-day trial for an agency's own site first. Do not promise an Agency-tier trial or a free multi-client rollout without checking eligibility. Approved affiliate program: 20% of eligible subscription revenue for the first 12 paid months, subject to attribution, exclusions, holds and payout terms. No self-referrals. Affiliates are not required to buy a customer subscription. Agency subscription and affiliate participation are separate options.

Live https://visitorping.com/affiliates and https://visitorping.com/affiliate-terms returned HTTP 200 and contained the rate and duration on September 10, 2026.

## Economics

Source: product packages/shared/src/plans.ts. Monthly Starter $19, Pro $49, Agency $149. At unchanged monthly Agency pricing, 6,712 accounts produce $1,000,088 gross MRR. This is arithmetic, not a forecast; annual discounts, mix, churn and costs change the economics. Agency supports up to ten sites; ten client sites are not ten paying accounts. Affiliate commission is not additional revenue.

## Delivery and evidence

The first inquiry uses Web Design Pros 365's explicitly invited technology-partnership form and its Partnership inquiry option. Receipt stored separately. Never infer delivery or a reply from a prepared draft. Do not rerun a submission after a timeout until its result is checked.

Resend is the configured transactional mail provider and prohibits unsolicited outreach: https://resend.com/legal/acceptable-use . Do not use it for this campaign. Normal business mailbox access is not currently connected. Other organizations' sales quote forms are not automatically partnership channels. Review the exact invitation before sending. Do not book founder calls, opt into subscriptions, or agree to paid services merely to contact someone.

## Measurement

Track researched → appropriate inquiry submitted → reply → trial → first real visitor and notification verified → paid → retained. Record timestamps and actual Stripe-derived MRR. UTM tags identify campaign links but are not proof of a signup-to-revenue attribution join. No campaign revenue has yet been verified. Earlier baseline was $0 MRR; refresh from Stripe before reporting a new current balance. Never count sample visits or bot traffic as acquired customers.

## Reply handling

If interested: send the existing signup/trial instructions and determine one site they control, platform, and whether the evaluator has an iPhone. Do not install on a client's site without that customer's authorization. If asking about partnership: send published affiliate terms and application. If not interested: close the record and stop contact. Do not promise lift in sales, visitor identity, white-label rights, or custom pricing.

## Outstanding work

Check the first receipt; connect a usable reply inbox before promising monitored follow-up. Verify remaining contact routes. Gather real pilot feedback and verified subscription outcomes. This folder and the local review page are preparation and execution records, not a deployed new public campaign or evidence of customer growth.
