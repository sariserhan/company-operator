VisitorPing: Website Doorbell is an iPhone companion for people who run websites and want to notice visits while they are away from their desk. It works with the VisitorPing web service: an owner installs the website tracker, connects their site, and uses the dashboard or iPhone app to follow incoming activity.

The iPhone app includes Live Radar across connected websites, visitor filters, page-by-page journeys, and configurable push alerts. An owner can inspect the page a visitor is viewing and the recorded traffic source instead of repeatedly opening a desktop analytics dashboard. The notification feature is intended to make existing activity visible; installing VisitorPing does not itself generate traffic or guarantee sales.

The app is available on the App Store for iPhone and requires iOS 16.4 or later. Its first public release was August 24, 2026; version 1.0.1 was released September 4. This is a submission of an existing app, not a claim that it launched today.

The companion app connects to a paid VisitorPing service. Website plans start at $19 per month, with an existing 14-day Starter trial. There is no permanent free website-tracking tier. We are not promising an unrestricted NFR license in this submission.

An interactive preview is available at https://visitorping.com/demo. It is clearly labeled as example data and simulates a notification in the browser; it is not footage of an actual iPhone push.
